About Us

Who are we?

Rumours circulate about the people behind NURK. Are they famous comedians trying to prove they are so funny that they can make it in the cartoon world even without their famous names?

People call us the Daft Punk of the cartoon world. (Let's be honest for a second: no one calls us the Daft Punk of the cartoon world, we are, however, looking on Ebay for those fancy motorcycle helmets.)

So, we cannot give you names, but we are an age-diverse, multiracial, co-ed group of comedians and cartoonists who bring out the best in each other. We publish that best under the name NURK.
